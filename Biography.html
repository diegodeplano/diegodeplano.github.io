Research interests

His expertise is the development of distributed control, optimization and learning algorithms for large groups of autonomous systems that interact within a communication or sensing network, with limited information availability and constrained resources, also when agents join and leave the network over time. His research interests include:

  • Open multi-agent systems: coordination, optimization and learning in networks whose agents change over time
  • Distributed optimization and online learning over networks, robust to asynchrony, communication failures and inexact computations
  • Consensus and dynamic tracking of time-varying signals and network parameters, such as size, diameter and eccentricities
  • Monotone and nonexpansive systems, contraction theory and monotone operator theory, with applications to recurrent neural networks
  • Distributed estimation and control of large-scale networked systems
  • Applications to multi-robot task allocation and routing, energy communities and buildings, and the agri-food industry
